Mehram — Sign in and the wali flow

Nineteen screens: the login everyone shares, how a wali joins, and what he sees once he is in.

Three decisions this set makes

Google and Apple sign in, but the phone number is still required.Social login is a convenience for returning users, not a replacement for the number. The phone is how a wali is invited, how families are reached, and how you stop one person holding several accounts. So Google gets you in, then L3 asks for the number once and links the two.
A wali is never charged and never verifies ID.He gives a name, a relationship and a phone number. She has already told us who he is, and asking a 60-year-old father for a CNIC scan is where he stops. Phone verification is enough.
W3 is the most important screen in this flow.Before he accepts, he reads exactly what he can do and what he cannot — in two plain lists. He can decline for her; he can never accept for her, and he never sees her photographs. Getting that in front of him before he agrees is what makes the role trustworthy on both sides.
