What has to come out of the menu

Most of the current items describe a browsing product. They contradict the screens you have just rebuilt, and a user who finds them will correctly conclude the privacy promises are decoration.

Remove Profile Views — implies people browse you freely. There is no browsing.
Bookmarks — you cannot save profiles you never browsed.
Hide from Discovery — Discover no longer exists.
Hide Distance — distance-from-you is a dating-app concept.
Blur Photos toggle — replaced by the four photo modes.
"Active today" — presence status invites checking who is online.
Hide Location toggle — city-only is now the default, not an option.
Add — several are launch blockers Delete account — required by Play, in-app and by web URL.
Download my data — expected wherever you sell.
Privacy policy, Terms, Refund policy — required for submission.
Membership and receipt — people need proof of what they paid.
Contact support — required, and families will call.
Found my match — the metric your whole business rests on.
Language — Urdu and English.
